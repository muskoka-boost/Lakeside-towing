What to have ready

You do not need any of this to call us — but having it to hand gets a truck moving faster and gets you an accurate price on the first attempt.

  1. Where you are. A cross street, a business name, a highway marker, or a fire number on a rural road. “Just past the bend” is not enough at night.
  2. The vehicle. Year, make, model and colour. Whether it is front, rear or all-wheel drive if you know.
  3. What happened. Will not start, will not steer, in the ditch, out of fuel, keys inside. This is what determines whether you need a tow, a roadside call or a winch-out.
  4. Where it is going. Your mechanic, a dealership, your driveway, or our storage yard.
  5. Whether you are safe. If you are on a live highway shoulder or the vehicle is in water, say so first — it changes how we prioritise the call.

If anyone is hurt

Call 911 first. Always. Police, fire and paramedics get there faster than we do and they are what an injury scene needs. Once everyone is safe and the scene is being managed, call us for the vehicle.

If your vehicle is in a live traffic lane and you cannot move it, stay belted inside with your hazards on rather than standing beside it.

Common questions

Are you open 24 hours?

Yes — 24 hours a day, seven days a week, including holidays. (249) 385-5240 reaches us at any hour.

How much does a tow cost?

An in-town tow — inside Orillia — typically runs $100 to $200. Anywhere outside Orillia is an out-of-town tow at $200 plus $3.50 per kilometre. Roadside calls — boost, lockout, fuel — are $80. Full list on our rates page, and you always get a firm price on the phone before a truck is dispatched.

What areas do you serve?

Orillia and the surrounding area, including Rama, Washago, Gravenhurst, Barrie, Cumberland Beach, Geneva Park, Grays Bay and the smaller communities around Lake Couchiching. We work our own patch rather than chasing jobs across the province. If you are not sure whether you are in range, call and ask.

What is the difference between in town and out of town?

Orillia is in town. Everywhere else we go — Rama, Washago, Gravenhurst, Barrie and the lakeside communities — is out of town, and a tow there is quoted at the out-of-town rate rather than the in-town range. Flat-rate roadside calls cost the same anywhere in our area.

Do you do long distance towing or enclosed transport?

No to both. We do not run long distance hauls across Ontario or into other provinces, and we do not operate an enclosed carrier. We are a local towing and recovery outfit working Orillia and the surrounding area. If your job needs either of those, call us anyway and we will tell you plainly that it is not ours.

Are you OPP approved?

Yes. We are approved for Ontario Provincial Police calls and hold Ministry of Transportation Vehicle Storage Certificate VS-212-186-623. We are fully licensed and insured.

Do you take credit cards?

Ask when you call and we will confirm what we can take for your job, so there are no surprises at the roadside.

Can I choose which tow company attends my collision?

In Ontario, yes. Even when an officer at the scene arranges a tow, you are entitled to choose your own tow operator and where your vehicle is taken. If you want us, say so, or call us directly.

Stuck right now?

One call, a real person, and a price before the truck rolls. We are open every hour of every day, including holidays.
