About us

Your trusted towing partner

Lakeside Towing & Recovery is a local, full-service towing company based in Orillia. Emergency towing, winch-out and recovery work, roadside assistance and MTO-licensed storage — across Orillia and the surrounding area, with a truck on the road at any hour.

“We Meet by Accident!”

It is written on the side of the truck, and it is the honest summary of this business. Nobody plans to meet a tow operator. By the time you are reading our number, something has already gone wrong — the car will not start, the tank ran dry, the keys are visible on the driver’s seat through a locked window, or there has been a collision.

What we can control is what happens next. That means a person answering the phone rather than a queue, a realistic arrival time rather than an optimistic one, and a price agreed before a truck is dispatched rather than presented once your vehicle is already on the deck.

Local, and that matters

Our yard is at 123 Norweld Dr, Orillia, ON L3V 7Z2. Not a mailbox, not a call-forwarding number — the trucks and the storage are both here. A great many towing numbers that surface in a search are dispatch brokers who sell your call to whichever operator bids for it, which is how people end up waiting two hours for a truck coming from somewhere else entirely.

Being based here also means we know the roads. Which stretches of Highway 11 hold snow when the forecast says the roads are fine. Which concession shoulders go soft in the spring thaw. Which cottage lanes off Rama Road stop being plowed in December. That knowledge is the difference between finding you quickly and driving past your turn twice.

What we actually do

24/7 emergency towing, winch-out and recovery, battery boosts, lockouts, fuel delivery, and secure indoor and outdoor vehicle storage. We serve Orillia and 15 other communities across Simcoe County and south Muskoka.

What we do not do is just as worth saying. We are not a long distance carrier — we do not run hauls to Toronto, Ottawa or Sudbury — and we do not operate an enclosed trailer for classics and exotics. We work our own patch. Orillia is in town; every other community we serve is treated as out of town and priced that way. If a job falls outside that, you will hear it from us on the first call rather than after a long wait.

We are approved for Ontario Provincial Police calls, which means we work collision scenes regularly and understand how they are managed. Worth knowing: in Ontario, even when an officer arranges a tow at a scene, you are entitled to choose your own tow operator and where your vehicle is taken.

Documentation

MTO Vehicle Storage Certificate

Certificate VS-212-186-623, issued by the Ontario Ministry of Transportation. It is what allows us to legally store vehicles, and it comes with obligations: proper records of what came in, when and in what condition, and specific rules about notifying owners and lienholders.

That paper trail is not bureaucracy for its own sake. When an insurer starts asking questions about a total loss, or an adjuster needs to establish when a vehicle arrived and in what state, the documented record is the difference between a clean claim and a slow one. An unlicensed lot keeping an eye on things has none of it.

How we work

Four things we hold to

The quoted price is the price

You hear a number on the phone before a truck is dispatched, and that is the number on the invoice. If something on scene genuinely changes the job, we tell you before we start — not after your vehicle is already loaded.

Honest arrival times

It is easy to say twenty minutes and turn up in ninety. We would rather tell you it will be an hour and be right. On a snowstorm night that honesty is worth more than optimism.

No surcharge for the hour

A boost at 3 a.m. on a holiday costs the same as a boost at noon on a Tuesday. Charging a premium for the hours when people are most stuck is not how we want to run this.

We say when we are not the answer

If a job needs a locksmith, a heavy-duty operator or a shop rather than a tow, we will tell you that on the phone. Sending a truck to a job we cannot finish helps nobody.

Stuck right now?

One call, a real person, and a price before the truck rolls. We are open every hour of every day, including holidays.
